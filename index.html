Welcome to Origins of Mathematics. Using selected points along the timeline of mathematics, we will explore mathematical ideas in the context in which they developed and learn to appreciate their cleverness and beauty. The website contains lesson notes, assignments, and course resources that support the course throughout the semester.


I hope these notes help someone discover the beauty of mathematics.
